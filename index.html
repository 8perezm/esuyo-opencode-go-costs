Get the most value from OpenCode Go

Every model on OpenCode Go ($10/mo), OpenCode Go Plus ($40/mo) and OpenCode Go Teams ($20/member/mo) comes with a different usage allowance, which makes them hard to compare. Token prices are the same on all plans — only the included monthly allowance differs. Change the numbers to match how you work. Prices and allowances are fetched live from the OpenCode Go models endpoint at https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models.

Tip: tick Project tokens to enter a workload and reveal the cost columns, then use Columns ▾ to add or hide columns such as Plus vs Go and Teams vs Go.

How to read: “Cost” is dollars of allowance consumed by the workload above (input at fresh/cached rates plus output at the full output rate). “Sub cost” is the pro-rata share of your subscription that workload burns: cost ÷ allowance × plan price (e.g. $3.73 on a $60 Go allowance = $3.73 ÷ $60 × $10 ≈ $0.62; on Go Plus the same workload against a $180 allowance = $3.73 ÷ $180 × $40 ≈ $0.83). “% of cash” = cost ÷ plan price ($10 for Go, $40 for Go Plus, $20/member for Go Teams; cash-equivalent). “% of allowance” = cost ÷ that model’s allowance for the selected plan (e.g. $3.73 on a $60 Go allowance = 6.2%, ≈16.1 blocks per month). Values >100% mean the workload does not fit in one subscription for that model.
Assumptions: “Total tokens” splits into input and output by “Output %”; output tokens are always charged at the full output rate (no cache discount on generated text). Prices are the standard rates reported by the Go models endpoint; models with peak-time multipliers get separate (Off-Peak) / (Peak) rows, and a scheduled price change is applied when it takes effect (tagged before then). Rows priced Free cost $0. This page stores no prices: it fetches the latest Go / Go Plus / Go Teams prices and allowances live from the OpenCode Go models endpoint on every load (the docs page, official Markdown, and text proxies are fallbacks when the endpoint or cross-site requests fail); successful fetches are cached in this browser for an hour for fast reloads. Press “↻ Refresh from source” to force a re-check. If OpenCode adds a new model, it appears here automatically on the next load. “Tokens / sub” is the total tokens the subscription’s allowance buys at the current hit rate and output share (allowance ÷ blended $/1M price; updates when you change either). “Plus vs Go” is how many times more tokens Go Plus buys than Go for that model (Go Plus allowance ÷ Go allowance; token prices are identical, so hit rate and output share cancel out). Free rows show “—”. “Teams vs Go” is the same comparison against the Go Teams allowance. Free rows show “—”. Tick “Project tokens” to enter a Total-tokens workload: it reveals the Total tokens field and the workload-derived columns (Sub cost, Cost, % of cash, Subs needed, Blocks per sub, % of allowance). Form selections (plan, search, tokens, hit rate, output %, free-models toggle, column visibility, sort column and direction) are remembered in this browser via localStorage.
