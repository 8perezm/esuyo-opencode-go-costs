Get the most value from OpenCode Go

Every model on OpenCode Go ($10/mo) and OpenCode Go Plus ($40/mo) comes with a different usage allowance, which makes them hard to compare. Token prices are the same on both plans — only the included monthly allowance differs. Pick a plan below and this page lines all models up against one everyday example: around 100 million tokens of coding work, where most of it reuses things the model has already seen. Change the numbers to match how you work — the whole table updates instantly, all right here in your browser.

How to read: “Cost” is dollars of allowance consumed by the workload above (input at fresh/cached rates plus output at the full output rate). “Sub cost” is the pro-rata share of your subscription that workload burns: cost ÷ allowance × plan price (e.g. $3.73 on a $60 Go allowance = $3.73 ÷ $60 × $10 ≈ $0.62; on Go Plus the same workload against a $180 allowance = $3.73 ÷ $180 × $40 ≈ $0.83). “% of cash” = cost ÷ plan price ($10 for Go, $40 for Go Plus; cash-equivalent). “% of allowance” = cost ÷ that model’s allowance for the selected plan (e.g. $3.73 on a $60 Go allowance = 6.2%, ≈16.1 blocks per month). Values >100% mean the workload does not fit in one subscription for that model.
Assumptions: “Total tokens” splits into input and output by “Output %”; output tokens are always charged at the full output rate (no cache discount on generated text). Tiered rows (context-size / peak / off-peak) are listed separately as in the official table. Beam Free = $0 (limited time). Prices are built into this page, so it works offline — just open the file. Press “↻ Refresh from source” to fetch the latest Go / Go Plus tables from the OpenCode Go pricing docs; the table updates in place and fresh prices are remembered for an hour. To update the built-in snapshot itself, edit the MODELS list in index.html (limit = Go, limitPlus = Go Plus). “Tokens / sub” is the total tokens the subscription’s allowance buys at the current hit rate and output share (allowance ÷ blended $/1M price; updates when you change either). Form selections (plan, search, tokens, hit rate, output %, free-models toggle, column visibility, sort column and direction) are remembered in this browser via localStorage.
